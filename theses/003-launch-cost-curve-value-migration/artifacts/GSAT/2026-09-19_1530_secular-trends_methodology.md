# GSAT × secular-trends — the largest revenue line is a cost reimbursement

## The finding

**GSAT's largest revenue line — wholesale capacity services, 62% of 3M 2026 revenue and 64% of 6M
— is filed as a reimbursement of the issuer's own network costs, and its entire year-on-year
movement is a timing artefact.** The filing states the cause directly:

> *"Wholesale capacity services revenue reflects revenue from the Customer under the Updated
> Services Agreements. The fluctuations in revenue during these three and six month periods
> related primarily to **the timing and amount of service fees associated with the reimbursement
> of network-related costs**."* `[📄 GSAT 10-Q p.34](https://agentii.ai/v/GSAT/sec166/34)`

**That single sentence decides the demand-side question this skill exists to ask.** The task's
mandate is *"is constellation cadence responding to the curve at all?"* At GSAT, **the dominant
revenue line is not a demand measurement at all** — it is a cost pass-through whose level moves
with the timing of network-cost reimbursement. **A revenue series that is mechanically a cost
series cannot answer a demand-side question, however precisely it is measured.**

**And the same filing states both signs of it in one clause:** *"decreased 5% and increased 9%,
respectively, for the three and six months ended June 30, 2026."* **The period basis inverts the
sign of the largest line, and the issuer files both.**

### 1.3 The margin swing is a revenue swing, amplified — and the amplifier changes sign with the basis

| Basis | Δrevenue | Δopex | Δoperating income | Leverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**This is the secular-trends result in its sharpest form.** At GSAT the operating result is a
**highly levered claim on revenue** — three-quarters of any incremental revenue dollar falls to
the operating line on the 6M basis, and on the 3M basis a $2.4 million revenue decline produced a
**$10.9 million** operating-income decline. **The demand-side trajectory is therefore not one input
among many at this issuer; it is very nearly the only one.**

**But the leverage itself is not estimable from this filing, because the two periods inside a
single 10-Q give opposite-signed and differently-shaped leverage.** +75.10% capture on one basis
and 4.60× amplification on the other are not the same parameter measured twice — they are two
ends of an unstable relationship. **Recorded as a DA-30 basis ambiguity in the demand-sensitivity
parameter itself, which is the fourth independent period-basis inversion at this ticker.**

**One-time items inside the 2025 comparative operating base, and they are filed.** GSAT received
**CARES Act employee retention credits of $2.0 million (February 2025) and $1.9 million (May
2025)**, recognised **as reductions to operating expenses** — $1.4M and $1.3M to cost of services,

**So the 3M 2025 comparative carries approximately $1.9 million of credit that reduced opex**, and
the 6M 2026 comparison is further affected by *"a noncash loss on disposal of assets recorded
during the first quarter of 2025 that did not recur in 2026."* **The +14.01% 3M opex increase is
therefore an increase measured against a credit-inflated base**, and the true ex-credit increase is
on the order of +10.6%. **Stated as a direction, not restated as a number** — the adjusted figure
is a derivation, not a filed line.

## 3. DA-23 at GSAT — the MIXED regime, four concepts, one of them a balance-sheet cell

**Every served `OperatingIncomeLoss` at GSAT is a positive of identical magnitude to a filed
negative — seven of seven periods, §1.1.** But the strip does not stop at the income statement:

| Concept | Cells | Served vs filed |
|---|---|---|
| `us-gaap:OperatingIncomeLoss` | **7** | positive served against a filed loss in 4 of 7 periods |
| `us-gaap:NetIncomeLoss` | **5** | incl. Q1 2025 served **+17,331,000** against filed `(17,331)` |
| `us-gaap:NetIncomeLossAvailableToCommonStockholdersBasic` | **5** | stripped on the same periods |
| **`us-gaap:RetainedEarningsAccumulatedDeficit`** | **3+** | 2026-06-30 served **+2,206,570,000** against filed `$(2,206,570)` thousand; 2025-06-30 served +2,126,269,000; 2024-06-30 served +2,087,861,000 |

**The fourth concept is a BALANCE-SHEET cell, and that is the generalisable result: DA-23 is not
an income-statement defect.** A guard built around the income statement will not see it.

**The regime is MIXED, and it is detectable from arithmetic alone.** Because positive and stripped
periods are interleaved, the served `|x|` stack is **not monotone**, and it fails in three places:
**Q2 2026 +4,775 greater than 6M 2026 +3,395**; **Q1 2025 +8,501 greater than 6M 2025 +2,355**;
**Q3 2024 +9,434 greater than 9M 2024 +3,300.** **In a UNIFORM regime — RKLB, PL, YSS — no such
violation can exist, because a uniformly sign-flipped stack is arithmetically consistent.** So the
monotonicity check is a **detector for MIXED regimes specifically, and it is silent by construction
on the UNIFORM ones.** GSAT is where it fires; PL, YSS and RKLB are where it cannot.

### 3.1 The fourth stripped concept proved from the equity identity alone

If `RetainedEarningsAccumulatedDeficit` were genuinely **+$2,206,570 thousand**, then with total
assets of **$2,444,812 thousand** `[📄 GSAT 10-Q p.6](https://agentii.ai/v/GSAT/sec166/6)` and

1. **DA-18 — "accepted for filing" is not a grant.** Sequential gates: ITU coordination, national

2. **DA-19 — an ITU filing is not a durable asset.** Priority can lapse for non-use. **A filing

constellation that has cleared a regulatory gate has not thereby bought a kilogram of payload.

**So the answer to the cadence question at GSAT is: the quantity does not exist, and the nearest
filed substitute is a queue position that says nothing about cadence.** This is a **weaker** result

replacement-only operator**. **At GSAT there is no cadence series to test elasticity on at all.**
Recorded as `NON-FORMABLE`, not as evidence.
