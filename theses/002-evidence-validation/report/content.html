The news: this thesis falsified its own pillar. PIL-5 asked whether at least half of 001's headline figures convert to DEMONSTRATED. Four of nine admissible readings fire; three more land exactly on the threshold with zero margin.

The reason is not that 001's numbers are wrong. It is that a converted figure is not a converted basis. A number can be reproduced exactly from filed cells and still not be the quantity its label names — off by 46.47 percentage points on a single SPCX ratio.

What this means for the programme. DEMONSTRATED is not a sufficient grade for an input. Any thesis from 003 onward that cites this validated set must carry the basis alongside the grade, or it inherits a figure that reproduces and means something else. That is the widest-reaching result in this report.

What it does not mean. 001's arithmetic largely held. Per-ticker sums reproduced at IRDM, MRCY, UTHR and SPCX. What failed is clearance and labelling — a narrower and more fixable defect than "the model is wrong."

Method note. The spec never states the denominator, so the ledger publishes every admissible reading rather than selecting one. A falsifier that passes or fails on an unstated denominator is not falsifiable — publishing the ladder is the finding.

How to read this report. The falsification and its ladder come first, because they are the news; the defect register follows, then the corrections census, then the tooling caveats a reader needs before trusting any figure above, and finally the instructions for 003–011 and the gaps that remain. The main line — "space is a cost curve, not a value pool" — is not overturned here; what this report moves is the evidentiary floor beneath it.

The Falsification · The Substantive Finding

A converted figure is not a converted basis

One ratio, two answers, no basis field. SPCX operating_margin is served as +29.79%. It is a filed −16.68%. The difference is 46.47 percentage points — and both numbers are arithmetically correct.

What happened. The served value divides an AI-segment six-month loss by consolidated revenue. It is a real computation on real filed cells. It is also not the operating margin of the company, and the artifact that serves it names no basis — which means the error is invisible to any check that recomputes the same way.

Why this is the thesis's central finding. Every other defect in this report produces a number that is wrong — catchable by magnitude, by sign, by plausibility. This one produces a number that is exactly right and answers a different question. It survives every check the programme currently runs, because the check and the number are computed the same way.

Implications · 003–011

What downstream theses must change

The one instruction: carry the basis, not just the grade. A figure graded DEMONSTRATED may still not be the quantity its label names.

1. A grade is not a licence to quote. Every input inherited from this validated set must name the basis it was computed on. Where the platform collapses two bases into one concept, the artifact must discover the second basis first — diligence alone cannot comply.

2. Recompute from components before using any operating figure. DA-23 affects 14 of 17 issuers. The remedy is the component identity shown in-line, and EPS × shares is not admissible as a sign test — it inverts in both directions.

3. Re-score 001's evidence count. Three issuers fall out of the "clean" denominator on testability grounds alone, so 001's quoted count should be restated as issuer-quarters where the test could actually run.
