## Open risks to this thesis

- **Evidence asymmetry.** The strongest technology claims in this sector are the
  least disclosed. SPCX reports mass to orbit and launches; nobody reports $/kg or
  radiator mass. Much of the baseline will be `MODELED` rather than `DEMONSTRATED`,
  which P4 permits but which must be labelled honestly.
- **Deal contamination.** Three of the highest-information names (IRDM, GSAT, RKLB)
  are in-flight deal securities under P11. Their operational disclosures remain
  usable as pre-merger baselines but must be tagged, and their post-close form is
  unknown.
- **Snapshot decay.** The universe coverage audit in the constitution is dated
  2026-09-18. Thirteen `NOT_READY` names may gain coverage and should be re-checked
  before thesis 002.
